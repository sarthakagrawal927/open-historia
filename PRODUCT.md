## Users and purpose

Strategy players explore emergent historical campaigns through natural-language orders, diplomacy and country memory. The immediate screen job is to inspect the map, issue an order, advance time, read the consequence and preserve the campaign.

Sources: AGENTS.md, PROJECT_STATUS.md and Site Health publicDirectory.projects.open-historia.purposeContract. The owner describes a local, credit-free strategy experiment inspired by Historia. It remains a held experiment; current hosted Free AI turns do not establish a historically accurate or finished simulation.

## Personality and design principles

Preserve the existing cartographic, restrained, terminal-like game interface. Amber marks action and story progress; the map and consequences carry the experience. Keep every campaign capability reachable with keyboard or touch. Country memory must survive interruption and reload. Do not turn the campaign into a generic dashboard or replace map context with decorative cards.

## Accessibility and inclusion

Maintain visible keyboard focus, readable controls, touch-sized actions and reduced-motion support. Small screens must reflow without overlapping controls. Browser emulation is evidence of layout, not physical-device qualification.
